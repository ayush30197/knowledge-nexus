# Homemade Pancakes

Making pancakes requires only a few simple ingredients and takes less than thirty minutes.

## Ingredients

- Flour
- Eggs
- Milk
- Baking powder
- Butter

## Instructions

1. Mix all dry ingredients.
2. Add milk and eggs.
3. Stir until smooth.
4. Cook on a hot pan until golden brown.

### Cooking Tip

Do not overmix the batter.

```text
Ideal Pan Temperature: Medium Heat
Cooking Time: 2-3 minutes per side
```

Enjoy your breakfast!
