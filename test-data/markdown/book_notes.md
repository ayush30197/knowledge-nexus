# Book Notes

Reading consistently improves vocabulary, critical thinking, and creativity.

## Key Takeaways

- Read every day.
- Take notes while reading.
- Revisit important chapters.

## Favorite Quote

> Small habits repeated consistently produce remarkable results.


Reading for even twenty minutes a day can compound into significant knowledge over time.
